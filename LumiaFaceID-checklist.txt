WORKING RULES
- Local work only. Do not push to GitHub or create a PR unless explicitly asked.
- For kernel experiments, use temporary boot only; never flash a test kernel.
- Never stream the iris and normal front camera together; they share CSIPHY2.
- After any emitter test, stop capture and verify torch_0, torch_1, flash_0 and flash_1 are all off.
- Never point IR at a person or eye; do not perform eye-directed tests. Windows operation does not establish that Android test conditions are eye-safe: Android pulse timing/current and optical exposure have not been independently validated. Do not treat driver limits or software timestamps as safety evidence.
- Do not test inside a metal can or reflective enclosure. Any bounded emitter check must use a confirmed diffuse target with no person or animal in the direct or reflected path.
- Keep the original FFU and private sensor profile.
- Do not use an IR pulse as a route-finding experiment; the output path must be identified first.
